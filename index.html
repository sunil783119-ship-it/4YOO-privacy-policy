4YOO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

4YOO is a hyperlocal marketplace that connects customers with nearby shops, shop owners and delivery partners. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use the 4YOO application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process information such as your name, phone number, email address, account details, delivery address, orders, cart items and transaction status.

2. Location Information

If you grant location permission, 4YOO may use your location to help identify nearby shops and support location-related features. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. You may also be able to enter an address manually where supported.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, display nearby products, process orders, coordinate delivery, provide customer support, process payments and refunds, maintain security, and improve the service.

4. Payments

Payments are handled through the payment methods and payment service providers made available in the app. Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. 4YOO does not ask users to share their payment PINs or OTPs with customer support.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with relevant shop owners, delivery partners, payment providers and technical service providers when necessary to fulfil orders, provide services, maintain security or comply with applicable law. We do not describe information as shared only when required by law if it is also shared to operate the service.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You can manage device permissions such as location through your device settings. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

4YOO is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
